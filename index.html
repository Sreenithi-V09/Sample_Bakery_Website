📅 Honest Shelf-Life Transparency

Manufacturing & Shelf-Life Index

Artisan bakery products do not share one universal lifespan. A fresh cream éclair must be enjoyed within 24 hours, whereas naturally fermented sourdough thrives for 5 days and dry butter biscuits for 2 weeks.

"Best-Before" Date (Peak Quality & Texture)

Applies to breads, croissants, and cookies. Indicates peak crunch, crust crackle, and chewiness. Consuming after this date remains safe when stored dry.

"Use-By" Date (Food Safety Strict Requirement)

Applies to fresh dairy cream, custard, and fresh berry cakes. For food safety reasons, these chilled confections must not be consumed past their strict use-by window.
